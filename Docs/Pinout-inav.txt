============================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
SERIAL / UART
------------------------------------------------------------
USB VCP   PA11 (DM)     PA12 (DP)    — OTG FS
UART1     TX: PA9       RX: PA10     — TELEM1
UART2     TX: PA2       RX: PA3      — DJIO3
UART3     TX: PD8       RX: PD9      — GPS
UART4     TX: PA0       RX: PA1      — TELEM2
UART6     TX: PC6       RX: PC7      — RC INPUT
UART7     TX: —         RX: PE7      — ESC telemetry
UART8     TX: PE1       RX: PE0      — TELEM3
SBUS_INV  PD0 (OUTPUT LOW)

------------------------------------------------------------
SPI
------------------------------------------------------------
SPI1      SCK: PA5    MISO: PA6    MOSI: PA7
SPI2      SCK: PD3    MISO: PC2    MOSI: PC3

CS pins:
  BMI270        CS: PA15   (SPI2)  — init 1MHz → 10MHz
  BMI088 Accel  CS: PD4    (SPI2)
  BMI088 Gyro   CS: PD5    (SPI2)
  AT7456E OSD   CS: PB12   (SPI1)

------------------------------------------------------------
I2C
------------------------------------------------------------
I2C1      SCL: PB6    SDA: PB7     — Connector ngoài
I2C2      SCL: PB10   SDA: PB11   — Baro + Mag (có PULLUP)

Sensors trên I2C2:
  Baro:  DPS310    addr 0x77 / 0x76
  Mag:   IST8310   addr 0x0E
  Mag:   QMC5883L  addr 0x0D

============================================================
GHI CHÚ CHO INAV TARGET
============================================================
- Chỉ dùng BMI270 (PA15/SPI2), bỏ BMI088
- SERIAL_PORT_COUNT = 9 (8 UART + 1 VCP)
- RC input: UART6 (PC6/PC7) + SBUS inverter PD0
- LED0_PIN = PE5 (green) hoặc PE6
- Motor TIM1 (M1-M4) + TIM3 (M5-M6) + TIM4 (M7-M10)
- USE_DSHOT_DMAR bắt buộc với H743
- I2C2 là internal (baro/mag), I2C1 là external connector
